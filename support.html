How far can you go?

How to play

Tap anywhere to turn. The ball rolls along a zig-zag path — turn at each corner to stay on it. Every correct turn is a point.

Lights

Lights ✦ appear on the path as you play. Roll over them to collect them, and spend them to continue a run after you fall.

Daily

Every day there is one new path, the same for every attempt. Score at least one point in a day's Daily to keep your streak going.
