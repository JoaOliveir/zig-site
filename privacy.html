ZIG

Privacy Policy · Last updated 6 October 2026

Short version

ZIG has no accounts and no analytics of its own. Your progress stays on your device. The only third party is Google AdMob, which serves the optional ad you can choose to watch to continue a run.

What stays on your device

Your best score, Daily scores, streak and Lights balance are saved locally on your device so the game remembers your progress. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads only appear if you choose to watch one to continue a run. To serve them, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your IP address (used to estimate a general location), device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), advertising data, interactions with ads, and crash and performance data. Google uses this to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud.

In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time with Privacy Choices on the home screen. On iOS the game asks for permission before using the advertising identifier; if you decline, ads are not personalised. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

ZIG is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. ZIG itself collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version handles personal data in a new way (for example an online leaderboard), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
