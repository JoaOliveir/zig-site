ZIG

Privacy Policy · Last updated 6 October 2026

Short version

ZIG does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What stays on your device

Your best score, Daily scores, streak and Lights balance are saved locally on your device so the game remembers your progress. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Third parties

ZIG contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Children

ZIG is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles personal data (for example ads or an online leaderboard), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
